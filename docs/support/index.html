Common questions

How do I start a Live Activity?

Enter your Sleeper username, choose your league, and load your matchup. Once background updates are connected, tap Start Live Activity. No Sleeper password is needed.

Why isn’t my Live Activity appearing?
  1. Check that Live Activities are enabled for SleeperLive in iPhone Settings.
  2. Open the app and check its background connection message.
  3. If shown, tap Retry background connection. If asked to wait, allow a minute before retrying.
  4. Once connected, load your matchup and tap Start Live Activity again.

If the problem continues, email support with the message shown in the app.

Why are scores delayed or different?

Network conditions and iOS can delay updates. Check the displayed update time and tap Refresh scores in the app. Saved scores can remain visible while offline. If a discrepancy continues after refreshing, send support the league, week, and time you noticed it.

What does “Proj.” mean?

Proj. values are experimental estimates, not Sleeper’s official live projections. A dash means an estimate is unavailable. Actual scores are displayed separately.

How do I change my league or username?

Open Settings to change your league. To use another username, log out and enter that username. Logging out clears the saved username and matchup and pauses updates, while preserving the device registration. It does not delete your Sleeper account.

How do I stop a Live Activity?

Tap Stop Live Activity in SleeperLive. Keep an internet connection available so the app can finish its server cleanup.

Why can’t the app find my matchup?

Check your username and selected league. A matchup may be unavailable if no opponent is assigned for the current week, or if Sleeper or the background service is temporarily unavailable. Try again, then contact support if the problem continues.

How do I ask about my data?

Email zimryquinonez@gmail.com with your question or data-removal request. Do not include passwords, push tokens, or device access credentials. Use Settings → Delete SleeperLive data to remove this device’s registration and activities. Logging out alone keeps that registration. Support can help with privacy questions and other records.
