Keep your fantasy football matchup close with SleeperLive, an independent companion for Sleeper.

YOUR MATCHUP AT A GLANCE
Enter your Sleeper username, choose a league, and see your team's score alongside your opponent's. No Sleeper password or separate SleeperLive login is needed. Your last selection is saved so you can return to your matchup.

FOLLOW FROM THE LOCK SCREEN
Enable background updates and start a Live Activity to follow your matchup while the app is closed. View scores on the Lock Screen and, on supported devices, Dynamic Island. Background updates are optional; you can also view and refresh scores inside the app.

KNOW WHAT YOU'RE SEEING
Check the last update time and warnings when scores may be outdated. Proj. values, when available, are experimental estimates rather than Sleeper's official live projections. Missing estimates appear as a dash.

TRY IT BEFORE CONNECTING
Explore a clearly labeled sample matchup without entering a username. Start a local preview Live Activity and simulate a score update to see how it works. Sample scores are fictional and do not receive live game updates.

CONTROL DEVICE ACCESS
Use Privacy & Help to contact support, disconnect your device, or request deletion of its registration and activity records from the active server database. Historical database copies, logs, and backups may remain; consult the privacy policy for retention details.

Live Activities must be enabled in system Settings. Score delivery depends on network conditions, iOS, and service availability; updates may be delayed. Real matchups require an available Sleeper fantasy football league and an assigned matchup for the current week.
